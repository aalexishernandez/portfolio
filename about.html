About Me

I’m a digital artist and student interested in how technology, culture, and visual storytelling connect. My work explores glitch aesthetics, surreal 3D environments, and web-based narratives.

In ART 74: Intro to Digital Media, I have experimented with:

  • Glitching images through Photoshop, Audacity, and TextEdit
  • Remixing memes and internet culture into new visual stories
  • Modeling and animating 3D objects in Blender
  • Creating HTML hyperlink narratives as net art

This portfolio is a snapshot of that learning process and how I’m starting to build a personal visual style with digital tools.
