About Me

I am a student interested in digital media, storytelling, and how technology shapes the way we see the world. In ART 74: Intro to Digital Media, I’ve worked with glitch techniques, memes, 3D modeling, and web-based art.

I like experimenting with different tools—Photoshop, Blender, HTML/CSS—and bringing them together to build surreal or playful images. I’m also curious about how online culture, identity, and everyday experiences can be turned into visual stories.

This portfolio collects a few of the pieces I made in class and shows how I’m starting to develop my own style in digital art.

Skills / Tools

  • Glitch processes using Photoshop, Audacity, and TextEdit
  • Remixing internet memes into new compositions
  • 3D modeling and basic animation in Blender
  • Building websites with HTML, CSS, and GitHub Pages
