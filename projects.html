My Projects

Glitch Art

This piece explores the beauty of digital errors. I used Photoshop and TextEdit to intentionally corrupt the image file, revealing unexpected colors, stripes, and fragments.

The project connects to glitch culture and the idea that mistakes or “broken” files can become their own aesthetic.

Meme Mashup

For this assignment I combined multiple current memes into a single composition. By layering text, screenshots, and images, the piece comments on how fast memes move and how we remix them to express humor and identity.

3D Object & Animation

This project is a 3D sculpture modeled in Blender. I experimented with lighting, materials, and camera angle to turn a simple object into something that feels surreal and dramatic.

The final result is both a still image and a short animation render.

HTML Hyperlink Narrative (Net Art)

In this earlier assignment I created a web-based story using HTML links, images, and text. The viewer clicks through different pages to move through the narrative like a hypertext adventure.
